# Design Specification — MAHSA: A Journey Written in Stars

## Visual Direction
- **Aesthetic**: Hybrid fantasy realism with deep atmospheric backgrounds, luminous gold highlights, and natural botanical details.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Midnight Sky (`#050816`, `#0b112c`, `#1c2b59`)
  - Enchanted Garden (`#061318`, `#0f2922`, Rose Pink `#f29bb4`, Crimson `#d94b72`, Ivory `#fbf8f1`, Muted Gold `#e8c987`)
  - Moonlit Lavender Climax (`#0d0b26`, `#231d47`, Silver Moon `#f4efe6`)
- **Typography**:
  - English Display: `Cinzel` & `Cormorant Garamond`
  - Persian RTL Message: `Vazirmatn` (`dir="rtl"`, `lang="fa"`, line-height `2.05`)
