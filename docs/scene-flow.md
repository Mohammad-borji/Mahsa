# Scene Flow & Interaction Architecture

1. **Scene 01 — The Beginning**:
   - Midnight sky with multi-depth twinkling stars, drifting clouds, distant crescent moon, and glowing horizon.
   - Opening quote + `BEGIN THE JOURNEY` CTA.
2. **Scene 02 — The Enchanted Garden**:
   - Winding stone pathway surrounded by interactive crimson/pink roses and white lilies, floating petals, fireflies, and butterflies.
   - Touching or hovering over flowers illuminates them.
   - Includes a subtle, unexplained crescent moon etched onto a garden stone (`#garden-secret-moon`) beside the right lilies.
3. **Scene 03 — The Golden Gate**:
   - Fantasy golden archway at the end of the garden pathway emitting warm white-gold light rays.
   - Reacts to pointer/touch and transitions through a bright golden light flash upon clicking `ENTER`.
4. **Scene 04 — The Birthday Celebration**:
   - Three-tier luxury cake adorned with roses, white lilies, and five flickering candles beneath `HAPPY BIRTHDAY MAHSA`.
   - Clicking `MAKE A WISH` brightens the candles, expands the golden aura, sends petals and fireflies outward, and elevates the camera toward the night sky.
5. **Scene 05 — The Moon and the Message**:
   - Large glowing crescent moon over a deep navy and lavender sky.
   - Reveals `MAHSA` followed by the line-by-line right-to-left Persian birthday message and final signature `Made with a little extra care. ✨`.
